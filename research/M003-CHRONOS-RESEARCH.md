# M-003 CHRONOS — Scientific Research Register
Revision: 1.0
Classification: LOCAL MARS in-app owner-only archive
Purpose: educational research and interactive relativity simulation.

## Scope
This record supports the CHRONOS Time Research Lab interface. It is not a construction manual for a time machine. No known technology can transport people into the past or open a traversable dimensional gate.

## Established physics
1. Special-relativistic time dilation: for inertial relative speed v, traveler proper time is τ = t sqrt(1 - v²/c²), with Lorentz factor γ = 1/sqrt(1 - v²/c²).
2. Gravitational time dilation: clock rates depend on gravitational potential; precision clocks and satellite navigation systems require relativistic corrections.
3. These effects allow different elapsed times between observers, but do not provide a practical method for returning to an earlier time.

## Speculative concepts
- Traversable wormholes: mathematical solutions in some theories; no observed or engineered example. Stability may require exotic conditions/negative energy, and there is no demonstrated method to create or control one.
- Closed timelike curves: appear in some mathematical spacetime solutions, but their physical realizability is unknown.
- Dimensional gates, matter transfer and human teleportation: no verified experimental evidence or engineering pathway.

## John Titor record
John Titor was an internet persona active in 2000–2001 who claimed to be a time traveler from 2036. The claims and predictions have not been independently verified. Mention of the IBM 5100 is a historical computer reference, not proof of time travel.
