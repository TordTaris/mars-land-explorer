# SECRET PROGRAM · PLANETARY CLIMATE & HABITABILITY DIRECTORATE
## LOCAL MARS — Internal concept charter

**Classification:** INTERNAL PROJECT CONCEPT — NOT A REAL-WORLD GOVERNMENT OR OPERATING AGENCY  
**Executive sponsor:** KING (LOCAL MARS project role)  
**Program scope:** Research and simulation of planetary climate, closed habitats, and long-term habitability.

## 1. Mission
Coordinate scientific research and engineering simulations for a safer, progressively more habitable Mars. The immediate achievable goal is reliable pressurized habitats with verified life-support systems. Planet-wide atmospheric engineering is a distant research question, not an operational capability.

## 2. Organization
- **Office of the KING / Program Council:** priorities, resource allocation, review gates, risk acceptance.
- **Atmospheric Science & Climate Observatory:** atmospheric composition, dust, radiation, temperature, circulation and climate models.
- **Life Support & Habitat Engineering:** oxygen generation, CO₂ removal, pressure management, thermal and humidity control, water recovery, crop modules.
- **Energy & Industrial Systems:** solar/nuclear power concepts, storage, thermal management, spare parts and industrial process safety.
- **Orbital & Surface Sensor Network:** simulated satellite constellations, weather stations, surface sensors, communications and data integrity.
- **Planetary Protection & Biosafety:** contamination prevention, environmental impact, biological containment and independent safety review.
- **Mission Assurance & Audit Office:** test plans, incident reporting, independent verification, configuration control and release approval.
- **Data, AI & Digital Twin Office:** model governance, telemetry provenance, uncertainty estimates, access control and simulation validation.

## 3. Current program status
**M-003 Atmospheric Engineering Lab — prototype simulation**
- Browser simulation for a sealed habitat: O₂, CO₂, pressure, temperature, humidity, nitrogen buffer, power reserve and safety alerts.
- Dome A is represented as the initial habitat; B agriculture and C emergency shelter are planning placeholders.
- The page is an educational model. It is not connected to physical sensors, valves, oxygen generators, satellites or habitat controls.
- Displayed readings and rates are illustrative, not engineering setpoints or certified life-support specifications.

## 4. Phased roadmap
### Phase 0 — Verify the digital foundation
- Validate routes, permissions, telemetry schema, audit logs, backup and deployment rollback.
- Mark simulated values clearly; distinguish measured, imported, estimated and synthetic data.
- Test alarm logic, sensor failure, loss of power, pressure leak, CO₂ scrubber failure and communication outage.

### Phase 1 — Closed habitat research
- Model pressure vessel, oxygen supply, CO₂ removal, trace contaminant control, humidity, thermal loops and water recycling.
- Add redundant life-support trains, isolation zones, emergency reserves and crew procedures.
- Require independent engineering review before any physical prototype.

### Phase 2 — Surface weather and resource observatory
- Model a distributed network of surface weather stations and orbital observations.
- Ingest validated public scientific datasets with source, timestamp, units and uncertainty.
- Build local forecasting and dust-storm warning simulations.

### Phase 3 — Multi-habitat settlement
- Simulate independent pressure zones, agriculture, water extraction and storage, energy microgrids, maintenance and logistics.
- Test inter-habitat resource sharing and safe isolation under failures.

### Phase 4 — Planetary climate research
- Research atmosphere, radiation, dust, volatile inventories, energy balance and planetary protection constraints.
- Compare peer-reviewed terraforming feasibility studies and quantify uncertainties.
- No attempt to release gases, organisms, contaminants or alter the real Martian environment is authorized by this project charter.

## 5. Governance and safety gates
1. Every feature must be labelled simulation, imported observation, or verified physical measurement.
2. No simulated reading may be presented as real atmospheric telemetry.
3. No life-support design is declared safe without qualified independent engineering validation and applicable standards.
4. Physical actuators and remote commands remain out of scope; no autonomous control of real equipment.
5. All changes require version control, test evidence, rollback plan and documented approval.
6. Protect planetary environments and follow applicable international space law and planetary-protection guidance.
