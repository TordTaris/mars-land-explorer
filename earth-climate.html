NASA POWER provides analysis-ready satellite-derived meteorological and solar datasets. Daily products are updated with a typical delay of a few days; they are not live ground-station telemetry.

Safety boundary: This is a read-only observation and forecast integration. It cannot seed clouds, release aerosols, alter rainfall, steer storms, control satellites, or change Earth's atmosphere. Forecasts are model outputs, not direct measurements. Any real weather-modification research or operation requires competent authorities, independent scientific review, environmental assessment and legal authorization.

Weather forecast source: Open-Meteo (multi-model forecast service). Climate reference: NASA POWER. LOCAL MARS displays these feeds for scientific context only. Data may be unavailable due to provider/network limits.
