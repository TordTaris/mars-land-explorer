02 · RESEARCH FILES — FACTUAL STATUS

ESTABLISHED · SPECIAL RELATIVITY

Moving clocks accumulate less proper time relative to an inertial observer, as measured in the observer's frame. Effects have been measured with precision clocks and particle experiments.

ESTABLISHED · GRAVITATIONAL TIME DILATION

Clock rates differ with gravitational potential. This is measured and must be accounted for in satellite navigation systems.

SPECULATIVE · WORMHOLES

Allowed as mathematical solutions in some models, but no traversable wormhole has been observed or shown to be physically constructible.

UNVERIFIED · JOHN TITOR

Internet persona from 2000–2001 claiming to be from 2036. No independently verified evidence establishes time travel or the identity behind the posts.
