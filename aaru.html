PROJECT PARAMETERS

A’ARU 1.2 · Integrated masterplan

A’ARU 1.2 is planned around a 10,000-person target on the expanded 1 km × 1 km M-001 site. The 245 m ring radius and 639 m visual base envelope are concept dimensions; habitable floor area, evacuation, shielding, structural loads, life-support closure and power budgets still require detailed engineering and independent validation.

Next engineering work: calculate usable residential and agricultural floor area, validate rotation comfort and structural loads, calculate shielding mass, model life-support closure rates, and size redundant power and emergency systems.
