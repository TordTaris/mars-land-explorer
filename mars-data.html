LOCAL MARS integration pipeline

Stage 01

Discover

Search PDS and mission archives; record product identifiers and archive links.

Stage 02

Validate

Check labels, coordinate systems, map projection, units, processing level, quality flags and license.

Stage 03

Normalize

Convert supported footprints to a common Mars GIS reference while preserving original metadata and provenance.

Stage 04

Publish

Serve approved derived layers to the LOCAL MARS map with source citations and uncertainty overlays.

Sources: NASA Planetary Data System (PDS), PDS Geosciences Node, Arizona State University THEMIS. Archive records may be historical; timestamps and product versions must be shown per item.
